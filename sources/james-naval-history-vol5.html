William James — Naval History of Great Britain, Vol. V (1793–1820)

Author: William James (d. 1827) Title: The Naval History of Great Britain, from the Declaration of War by France in 1793, to the Accession of George IV Edition: A New Edition, with Additions and Notes (London: Richard Bentley, 1860) OCLC: 51212751 Volume: Vol. V (of 6) Period covered: 1809–1813 Raw text: wiki/raw/james-naval-history-vol5.txt (27,835 lines)

Description

Volume V of William James’s Naval History of Great Britain covers the years 1809 through 1813. It opens in continuation of 1809 with a lengthy account of light squadrons and single-ship actions, then proceeds year by year through the remainder of the Napoleonic naval war and the opening of the War of 1812. The volume includes a final section on the state of the British and American navies in 1813, followed by an appendix containing annual abstracts of the Royal Navy’s ships and vessels with explanatory notes.
